Experience highlights

Open-source software

I lead development of aweSOM, a CPU/GPU-accelerated Python package that combines self-organizing maps and statistical ensembles to detect anomalies in large, high-dimensional datasets.

High-performance computing

As principal investigator, I manage an NSF ACCESS allocation. I have experience running multi-node distributed simulations on supercomputing clusters and broad knowledge of distributed systems (MPI, Slurm, PBS).

Methods and data systems

I build workflows for simulation outputs that contain more than 10 million data points across thousands of snapshots. I also build inference pipelines for stellar and spectroscopic survey data.

Mentorship and collaboration

I mentored students and early-career researchers on astrophysics projects that led to peer-reviewed publications. I also led collaborations across simulation, theory, and observations.
